# Anti-AI Luxury Editorial Design System

## 1. Principles
- **No Generic AI Aesthetics**: Avoid flat, sterile, corporate-minimalist cards with default purples or blues.
- **Editorial Typography**: Pair classical high-contrast serif (Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display) with warm humanist sans-serif (Montserrat, Inter) and romantic handwriting accents (Pinyon Script, Great Vibes).
- **Organic Color Harmony**:
  - Warm Alabaster & Cream: `#FDFBF7`, `#F7F4EE`
  - Champagne Gold & Muted Ochre: `#D4AF37`, `#C5A059`, `#E6CA65`
  - Botanical Sage & Eucalyptus: `#556B2F`, `#8A9A5B`, `#4A5D4E`
  - Rose Dust & Terracotta: `#D9A59F`, `#C47D68`
  - Deep Velvet Charcoal: `#1A1A1A`, `#2D2B2A`
- **Tactile Materiality**:
  - Wax seal stamps with metallic specular highlights and micro-embossing.
  - Subtle gold foil shimmer effects.
  - Deckled edge paper textures and soft ambient shadows (`0 20px 50px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.15)`).
- **Poetic Microinteractions**:
  - Interactive wax seal breaking with haptic/chime audio.
  - Unfolding letter physics.
  - Floating botanical petals.
